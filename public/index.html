How booking is protected
  • Overlapping reservations are rejected, including reservations by the same client.
  • Critical damage blocks bookings until all critical incidents are repaired.
  • An active deployment stays locked until completion, even if overdue.
  • Adjacent windows are allowed: a 14:00 return can be followed by a 14:00 reservation.
